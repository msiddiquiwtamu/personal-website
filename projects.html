Affective computing

Multimodal Emotion Recognition & VIRI

What can a computer learn about emotion from more than one signal? My doctoral research combined speech with visible and infrared facial imagery and led to the VIRI database.

Research · Computer vision

Immersive learning

Molecular Lens AR

An augmented-reality approach to exploring molecular structures and scientific ideas in three dimensions, developed with students in the context of computational materials science education.

Collaborative research · AR/VR
03 / 06
Human-centered AI

AI Tools for Rural Healthcare

Exploring how language models and retrieval-based systems could support rural nurse practitioners. The work combines a needs assessment with early decision-support prototypes.

Collaborative research · LLMs
04 / 06
Robotics & interaction

A.U.R.A. — Mixed-Reality Robotic Arm

A student-led interdisciplinary project connecting a Meta Quest 3 interface to a six-axis robotic arm. A hands-on experiment in translating virtual interactions into physical movement.

Student project · Robotics
05 / 06
Sensing & the environment

TerraTek Environmental Monitoring

A student team developed a network of environmental sensors and a web dashboard to observe rainfall, water levels, and related conditions at a rainwater-harvesting site.

Student collaboration · IoT
06 / 06
Learning by building

Immersive Calculus Learning

Exploring whether interactive three-dimensional environments can help students understand abstract mathematical ideas, including solids of revolution, through hands-on visualization.
