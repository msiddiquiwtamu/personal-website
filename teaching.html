Teaching
IN THE CLASSROOM

Learning happens
when ideas come alive.

I enjoy teaching computer science because it gives me the opportunity to explore ideas alongside students. Beyond understanding the theory, I want students to see how concepts work, ask questions, experiment, and create things of their own.

“There's something especially satisfying about seeing an idea turn into a working program.”

— A thought on teaching

BEYOND THE LECTURE

Learning by building.

One of my favorite parts of teaching is working with students as their projects move from an initial idea toward something that actually works. In senior capstone and independent research, the learning often comes through exploration: trying an approach, discovering what doesn't work, and figuring out what to change.

Those projects have led us into virtual reality, artificial intelligence, robotics, computer vision, and many other areas.
